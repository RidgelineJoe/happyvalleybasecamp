Come for the event. Stay for the space.

Clean, freshly remodeled 1903 farmhouse on a private 5 acres near Gray's Woods. This is the base to be close to PSU concerts, games, and events, with a little more space to enjoy the outdoors.

The land

Ample parking, a fire pit, grill, spacious lawn, native landscaping, and a small creek through the property. Room to spread out, and on game day you're in and out without fighting downtown traffic.

The house

A spotless chef's kitchen with a propane range and butcher block island. The bright, open dining area flows into a relaxed living space made for meals and time together.

Three bedrooms: two queens and two twins.

Workspace

A sit/stand desk with monitors in the corner of the dining room, on 500+ Mbps WiFi (40 Mbps upload). Bring your laptop, plug in and work, then step outside onto five acres when you're done.
